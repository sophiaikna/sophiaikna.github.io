I play the Viola in the Crescenta Valley High School Symphony Orchestra and the Pasadena Youth Symphony Orchestra Philharmonic. Displayed in the image to the left is our Philharmonic playing Stravinsky's Firebird Suite at our December concert. I have been given the privilege of playing works such as Tchaikovsky Symphony No. 2, Dvorak Symphony No. 7 and more! I especailly enjoy playing in chamber groups with my peers and also teaching Viola to younger students!

I was invited on my school's biannual France Trip, where I was given the opportunity to practice my French! Pour les personnes qui parlent français (ou qui aimeraient apprendre avec moi), nous sommes allés en Belgique, à Paris, à Nice, au Maroc et à Genève. Ma partie préférée du voyage était la Belgique, parce qu'il y a un travailleur très gentil dans une chocolaterie, qui a appris à nos amis l'accent belge du français!

I am currently the President of Sending Sunshine, a club at Crescenta Valley High School that writes kind cards and letters to senior citizens in the community. Recently, we have written over 600 cards and partnered with over 8 senior centers in the area! We additionally have raised over $600 to donate to senior centers in our community. The image to the left shows a portion of our club at the Sparr Heights Community Center, where we were able to deliver 300 cards written by our members.
