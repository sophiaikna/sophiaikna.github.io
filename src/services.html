In-Person tutoring sessions are held at a location of your choice. Either the student's house, the library, or our location in Pasadena! In-person tutoring sessions are a great choice for students who would like to stay extra-focused and want to get off of their screens for a bit.

Online tutoring sessions are held on Google Meet, where I am able to share a whiteboard for myself and the student to collaborate on working through problems, studying for exams, and more. Additionally, through this virtual platform, I am able to share a multitude of resources to the student for them to use during and after our sessions to ensure proficeincy in the subject we are working on!
